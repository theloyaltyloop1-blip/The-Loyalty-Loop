For shop owners

Give your regulars a reason to return.

You know their order. The Loyalty Loop helps you reward their loyalty: choose rewards, record visits from your phone, and see how your programme is doing. No paper cards to print, no extra scanner to buy.

The pitch in one breath

A free digital loyalty card for your shop, running from the phone you already own.

Customers join by scanning a QR code at the till. You scan their code, a stamp is added, and when the reward is earned they show you a redemption code. Free for Business, no subscriptions, no hardware.

How it works

1

Set up your shop

Add your address, pin your spot on the map, write a short description and choose your reward. Live to nearby shoppers in minutes.

2

Put the QR poster by the till

Print it from the app (Shop tab, QR poster). Customers scan it with their phone camera to join.

3

Record every visit

Scan the customer's QR code, or type their short code. Any phone or tablet with a camera will do.

4

Hand over the reward

When the card is full, the customer shows a redemption code and you give them what they've earned.

Why shops like it

Free to use

No subscriptions and no in-app purchases for the Business app.

Your rewards, your rules

Stamps, spend-to-earn, points or tiers. Or describe your own idea in plain words, such as "3 points is £1 off", and the AI programme builder sets it up.

Works without signal

Stamps are saved on the phone when the connection drops and sent later.

Bring regulars back

Automatic win-back emails to customers who have drifted away, and a birthday reward: bonus stamps or points, or a free item you name.

Your own card design

Design the card shoppers see, with templates and a colour wheel, so it feels like your shop.

Simple coaching

Weekly wins, watch-outs and next steps drawn from your own visits, reviews and reward activity.

Onboarding checklist

Send this to a shop that has said yes.

Before the first customer

1. Download The Loyalty Loop for Business and tap "New here? Set up your shop".
2. Add your address and pin your location.
3. Write a short description and choose your reward.
4. Design your card (optional).
5. Print the QR poster and put it by the till.

On the first day

1. Show the team how to scan a customer's code, or type the short code.
2. Ask each regular: "Have you joined our loyalty card? It's on the poster."
3. Record a stamp at every visit.
4. Check the app at the end of the week.

Answers to common questions

How much does it cost? Nothing. The Business app is free, with no subscriptions or in-app purchases.
Do I need special hardware? No. Any phone or tablet with a camera.
What can I offer? Stamps, spend-to-earn, points or tiers, or your own idea described in words.
What about customer data? We don't sell data. Details are in the Privacy Notice in the app.

Shared brand

Looks like the real thing.

The pack uses the same colours, type and tile style as the apps and website. Follow these rules for anything new.

Logo

Full logo (website asset)
Logo on warm paper
Mark only (small sizes, avatars)

Do

  • Use the logo files exactly as supplied. Resize only.
  • Keep clear space around it equal to the mark's width ÷ 4.
  • Use the mark alone for anything smaller than about 64 px.

Don't

  • Recolour, stretch, rotate, outline or add effects.
  • Redraw it or ask AI to "improve" it.
  • Put it on busy photos or low-contrast colours.

Palette

Tiles stay coloured: sage, mist, amber and indigo. Never make them white, grey or all one colour. In the Business app, tiles carry a 2px dark border (used in the shop-owner section above). The logo's own olive and orange belong to the logo only.

Type

Every visit, counted.

Inter for headings and body (medium weight, tight tracking on headings). Instrument Serif Italic for one or two emphasised words in a headline, as on the website.

Tone of voice

Warm and plain

Talk like a friendly neighbour. Short sentences, everyday words. "Show your code", not "present your credential".

Local and independent

Name the shops, the high street, the regulars. We are not a big-chain scheme.

Honest

Only say what the app does today. No made-up numbers, quotes or testimonials. Don't promise savings or sales.

Say

  • Your high street, in your pocket.
  • A reason to return.
  • Every visit, counted.
  • Free for shoppers. Free for shops.

Avoid

  • "Revolutionary", "game-changing", "disrupt"
  • Jargon: "engagement", "retention funnel"
  • Guaranteed results or invented statistics
  • Pressure: "last chance", "act now"

# 10-reel content calendar

Audience: local business owners (cafes, barbers, salons, bakeries) and their shoppers. 20-35 s each, 9:16. Pattern borrowed from the source reel: **hook in the first 3 to 7 seconds, one idea per ~6 s beat, comment-keyword CTA**.

Suggested rhythm: 3 per week, owner reels Tue/Thu, shopper reels Sat. Keywords: `LOOP` (owner reels, reply with the business sign-up link), `CARD` (shopper reels, reply with the app link), `DEMO` (offer a walkthrough). Replace `[X]` placeholders only with real figures. Shot IDs (S1, R2...) refer to `02-screen-recording-shot-list.md`.

---
## Reel 1: App demo for owners ("your whole loyalty scheme in a minute")
- **Hook (0-3 s):** fast cuts of the shopper card, the scan screen, the reward pop-up, then straight to camera.
- **On-screen text:** "Paper stamp cards, but they work." / "Set up in minutes" / "Comment LOOP"
- **Voiceover:** "If you run a shop, here's your whole loyalty scheme in one minute. A customer shows a code. You scan it. They get a stamp. When they've earned a reward, the app tells them, and tells you. No cards to print, no cards to lose."
- **Shots:** S1, R2, S4, S5, R3.
- **CTA:** "Comment LOOP and I'll send you the sign-up link."

## Reel 2: Why customers don't come back
- **Hook:** "Your customers loved you. So why didn't they come back?"
- **On-screen text:** "Nice coffee, nice chat, never seen again" / "Nobody gave them a reason" / "Give them a reason"
- **Voiceover:** "Most people don't leave because you did something wrong. They forget. Another place is closer, or just top of mind. A reason to return, like being one stamp from a free coffee, beats a flyer. Here's how to build that in an afternoon."
- **Shots:** to camera or B-roll of a quiet till; S1 (card one stamp from reward); R3.
- **CTA:** "Comment LOOP for the link."
- Do not quote retention statistics unless you can cite a real source.

## Reel 3: Stamp card vs app
- **Hook:** "Paper stamp cards are costing you customers."
- **On-screen text:** split screen "Paper: lost, forgotten, wet" vs "App: always in the pocket" / "You see who comes back"
- **Voiceover:** "Paper cards live in coat pockets. The app lives on the phone they're already holding. And with paper you never learn who your regulars are. With the app you see your members, who is close to a reward, and who has drifted away."
- **Shots:** a blank prop paper card; S1; R4.
- **CTA:** "Comment CARD to try it as a customer, LOOP if you own the shop."

## Reel 4: Win-back (the quiet customers)
- **Hook:** "Where did your regulars go?"
- **On-screen text:** "Haven't visited in a while" / "One nudge" / "Back through the door"
- **Voiceover:** "Every shop has customers who used to come weekly and then stopped. The Loyalty Loop shows who has gone quiet, and you can send them a friendly reason to return."
- **Shots:** R4 (lapsed seed members), R5, S7.
- **CTA:** "Comment LOOP."
- Before filming, confirm what the live app can actually send today (the feature list mentions win-back emails with a coupon and shop announcements). Film only what is live.

## Reel 5: Shop owner testimonial format
- **Hook:** the owner's strongest real sentence, in their own words, in the first 3 s.
- **On-screen text:** owner and shop name (with permission), the quote, "Using The Loyalty Loop since [month]".
- **Voiceover:** the owner's own voice. Three prompts: "What were you doing before?" "What changed?" "What would you tell another owner?"
- **Shots:** 15 s of the owner at their till; cutaway of their real shop card on a phone; shop door.
- **CTA:** "Comment LOOP."
- Must be a real owner who agreed in writing. No actors, no invented quotes.

## Reel 6: Behind the scenes
- **Hook:** "I'm building a loyalty app for local shops. This is what today looked like."
- **On-screen text:** "Day [N]" / a real screen / "Tell me what to build next"
- **Voiceover:** "Today I changed [one real thing]. A shop owner told me [real feedback], so I [real change]."
- **Shots:** screen recording of the real change; to camera.
- **CTA:** "Comment what your shop needs and I'll look at it."

## Reel 7: Shopper POV, "the card you can't lose"
- **Hook:** "Count your loyalty cards. Now count how many you've got on you."
- **On-screen text:** "One app, every shop" / "Find shops near you" / "Comment CARD"
- **Voiceover:** "Every cafe wants you to carry a card. You won't. Put them in one place, see how close you are to a free one, and find new shops near you."
- **Shots:** S1, S2, S6, S8.
- **CTA:** "Comment CARD for the app link."

## Reel 8: Setup speedrun for owners
- **Hook:** "Setting up a loyalty scheme for a coffee shop, timed."
- **On-screen text:** timer in the corner; steps "Shop details", "Reward", "QR ready"
- **Voiceover:** "Name the shop. Choose the reward. Choose how many stamps. Done. Then put the code on the counter."
- **Shots:** R1, R7 with a test shop. Show the real elapsed time you measure.
- **CTA:** "Comment LOOP."

## Reel 9: Myth bust, "loyalty is for big chains"
- **Hook:** "You don't need to be a chain to have a loyalty scheme."
- **On-screen text:** "Built for one-shop owners" / "Faces and names win"
- **Voiceover:** "Local shops win on faces and names. A stamp card is just a way of saying we noticed you came back. This is that, without a till upgrade."
- **Shots:** to camera; R3; S3.
- **CTA:** "Comment LOOP."

## Reel 10: The reward moment
- **Hook:** the reward unlock, slowed, from the first frame.
- **On-screen text:** "Stamp 10." / "Free coffee." / "That's the whole idea."
- **Voiceover (short):** "The best part of a loyalty scheme is the moment a customer earns it. Here it is."
- **Shots:** S4, S5, R2, R6.
- **CTA:** "Comment CARD or LOOP."

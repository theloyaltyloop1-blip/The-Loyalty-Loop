## How privacy is enforced (code, not promises)

- Key stays in function secrets. The app never sees it. JWT verification stays on.
- The user id comes only from the verified JWT. The context builder drops any membership row whose `user_id` differs, even if a query were wrong (tested).
- The model gets: this user's own cards and ready rewards (no first name, email, phone or id), up to 8 public approved shops (short refs `s1..s8`, never uuids, distance as "1.2 miles", never coordinates), the static FAQ. Business: aggregate stats only; the function now refuses (400) any stats payload containing keys such as email, name, phone, address, user id at any depth (tested).
- Location: the app rounds to 2 decimals (about 1 km) before sending, and the function rounds again.
- No chat is stored; the sheet keeps history in memory and clears it on close. Function logs are `{outcome, ms}` only.
- Output: JSON `reply` goes through the WhatsApp bot's `checkBotAnswer` (no links, emails, phone numbers, "as an AI"; 700 chars), shop ids must be among those offered (max 3), else a safe fallback line.
- Abuse: blatant injection or "who else shops here" is refused before any model call. The model is told to refuse off-topic, legal, medical, tax, money, other people, and instruction-reveal requests. Shop descriptions are passed as JSON data and the output filter removes links.
- Limits in order: minute (6) then day (20) then overall cap. A user stopped by the minute limit does not use the daily or overall counters. Kill switch: secret `WHIRL_ENABLED=false` makes both functions answer 503 and the sheet shows "resting".

### Privacy Notice paragraph (UK GDPR), for `privacy-notice.pdf` and the website

> **Ask Whirl (AI assistant).** If you choose to use Ask Whirl, the question you type is sent to our AI service provider, Groq, Inc., in the United States, so that it can write an answer. To answer questions about your own loyalty cards, we also send the names of the shops you have joined and your progress and rewards at those shops; for business accounts we send summary totals about your shop (never customer names or contact details). We do not send your name, email address, phone number, account identifier or precise location. We do not store your conversation: it exists on your device while the chat is open and is discarded when you close it. We keep only anonymous counts of how often the service is used, to keep it within limits and control cost. Our lawful basis is providing the feature you ask to use (contract) and our legitimate interest in running it safely. The transfer to the United States is protected by [the transfer mechanism in our agreement with Groq: owner to confirm, e.g. the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the IDTA/UK Addendum]. You can stop using Ask Whirl at any time, and hide it from your home screen in Settings. Ask Whirl can give wrong answers, and nothing it says is legal, financial or medical advice.
